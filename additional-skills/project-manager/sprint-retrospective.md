# Skill: Sprint Retrospective & Velocity

## Metadata
- **Owner**: Sandy Cheeks (Project Manager)
- **Slug**: sprint-retrospective
- **Purpose**: Velocity tracking, blocker analysis, and actionable sprint improvement action items.

## Operational Rules & Guidelines
1. **Data Over Speculation**: Base reviews on completed tasks in `tasks/done/`, bug counts, and cycle times.
2. **Three Core Pillars**: Keep retrospective structured around: What went well, What went poorly, What will we change next sprint.
3. **Actionable Commitments**: Every retro must produce 1-3 concrete, measurable action items with assigned owners.

## Procedures
- Step 1: Count completed tasks in `myemployee/tasks/done/` (with fallback to legacy subdirectories).
- Step 2: Calculate ratio of planned work vs urgent bug interruptions.
- Step 3: Publish retro notes to `/myemployee/RETRO-DDMMYYYY.md` and report to Bos.
